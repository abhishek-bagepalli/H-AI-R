
Leave Policy and Guidelines:

1. Types of Leave:
   - Casual Leave: Up to 3 days per year.
   - Sick Leave: Up to 10 days per year. Requires medical certificate for more than 2 days.
   - Earned Leave: Accumulated monthly, can be carried forward or encashed.
   - Maternity Leave: Up to 26 weeks, as per company and legal guidelines.
   - Paternity Leave: Up to 10 days within 3 months of childbirth.

2. Leave Application Process:
   - Submit leave requests via the HR portal at least 2 working days in advance.
   - Emergency leave must be notified to your manager via email or call.
   - Manager approval is mandatory.

3. Payroll Implications:
   - Unapproved leave may result in Leave Without Pay (LWP).
   - Accumulated leave balance is shown in monthly payslips.

Contact hr-leave@company.com for any queries.

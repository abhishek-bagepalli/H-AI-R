
Onboarding Process Overview:

1. Pre-Joining Formalities:
   - Submit signed offer letter and background check documents.
   - Upload documents to the onboarding portal.

2. First Day Checklist:
   - Welcome kit and ID card will be issued.
   - Introduction meeting with manager and HR.
   - Account credentials for Slack, Email, and Confluence provided.

3. Tools and Access:
   - Access to Jira, GitHub, and internal tools provided by IT.
   - Escalate access issues to it-support@company.com.

4. Orientation Program:
   - Two-day orientation covers company culture, policies, and benefits.
   - Attendance is mandatory.

Email onboarding@company.com for help.
